---
title: "Sharing themes"
description: "Try another starter theme, and move a site's whole look from one space to another as a theme file."
---

A theme file holds a site's whole look: the theme, the designs of blocks, pages, layouts and menus, and the site settings. It does not hold your content, your domains or your images. You find the buttons for it on the design overview, in the folded section **Theme gallery, import and export** near the bottom.

## Try another starter theme

1. Click **Design** in the sidebar and open **Theme gallery, import and export**.
2. Click **Theme gallery**.
3. Pick a theme. The buttons on top show only **Professional**, **Creative** or **Personal** themes; each card shows a preview and what it brings.
4. Leave **Replace designs this space already has** off to only add designs you have not made yet, or switch it on to replace yours.
5. Click **Apply**.

The twelve built-in themes:

| Group | Themes |
| --- | --- |
| Professional | Neutral, Corporate, Minimal, Practice |
| Creative | Bold, Studio, Playful, Noir |
| Personal | Editorial, Journal, Blossom, Terminal |

Everything arrives as drafts. Look the designs over, then publish them from the overview. If you do not like it, undo a change in its designer or restore a version (see [Publishing and preview links](./publishing.md#versions)).

## Export a theme

1. Click **Design** in the sidebar and open **Theme gallery, import and export**.
2. Click **Export theme** and choose **Published designs** (what visitors see) or **Drafts, as they are now**.

A `.json` file downloads, named after the space. Custom code from the site settings is only included if you may edit custom code.

## Import a theme

You need the permission **Edit designs**.

1. Click **Design** in the sidebar, open **Theme gallery, import and export** and click **Import theme**.
2. Under **Theme file**, choose a `.json` file exported from any Manablox space.
3. The dialog lists every design in the file and what happens to it: **New**, **Replaces** or **Skipped**.
4. **Replace designs this space already has** is on by default. Switch it off to keep your own designs where both exist.
5. Untick any design you do not want.
6. Click **Import as drafts**, then **Done**.

Good to know:

- Block and page designs find the matching block and content types by name, even when the other space built them separately. Designs for types this space does not have are skipped.
- Images and uploaded fonts are not in the file. If the designs use some that this space does not have, the dialog tells you; upload them again and pick them in the designers.
- Nothing is live until you publish.

To move a whole space, content and designs together, use [Moving a space](../admin/transfer.md) instead.
