---
title: "Invitations"
description: "Invite people by email into one or more spaces, let them pick their own password, and resend or revoke invitations that are still open."
---

An invitation is the easiest way to bring someone in: you type their email address and the spaces they should join, and they set up their account themselves, with a password nobody else knows.

## Who can invite

| Who | From where | Into which spaces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An administrator | **Settings > Users** | Any spaces, or none for an account without spaces |
| An owner or admin of a space | **Settings > Members** of that space | That space |

Anyone whose role may add and remove members of a space can invite into it.

## Inviting someone

From a space:

1. Go to **Settings > Members** and click **Invite** at the top right.
2. Type the person's **Email**.
3. Choose their **Role** in this space.
4. Click **Send invitation**.

From **Settings > Users** (administrators only):

1. Click **Invite** at the top right.
2. Type the person's **Email**.
3. Tick every space they should join and choose a role next to each one. Leave all unticked to give them an account without spaces.
4. Click **Send invitation**.

You should see the message "Invitation sent to" followed by the address. The person gets a mail "... invited you to Manablox" with a link. The link works once and for seven days.

:::note
If your installation cannot send mail, no mail goes out. The dialog shows the invitation link instead: click **Copy** and send the link to the person yourself, in a safe way. It is shown only this once.
:::

## Accepting an invitation

The person opens the link from the mail. The page shows who invited them and into which spaces.

- **Someone new** types their name and a password of at least 12 characters twice, then clicks **Create account and join**. They are signed in right away and see the spaces they were invited to.
- **Someone who already has an account** with that address clicks **Sign in**, signs in as usual, and then clicks **Accept invitation**. The spaces are added to their account.

If they are signed in with another account, the page offers **Switch account**.

## Open invitations

Invitations that were not accepted yet are listed under **Invitations**, below the members of a space, and below the accounts under **Settings > Users**. Each shows the address, the role or spaces, and until when it is valid. An expired invitation is marked **Expired**.

- **Resend** sends a new link and makes the old one stop working. Use it when the mail got lost or the invitation expired. Without mail, the new link is shown to copy.
- The trash button **revokes** the invitation: the link stops working. You can invite the person again later.

## When the space is full

If your plan limits how many people may use a space or the installation, Manablox checks the limit twice: when you invite, and again when the invitation is accepted. When the limit is reached, inviting says so, and accepting fails with a message and changes nothing. Once a seat is free again, the same link works.
