---
title: "Messages, read-only and suspended"
description: "The banners and help links your hosting provider can show in the admin, and what a read-only or suspended installation means for you."
---

If your installation is hosted for you, the provider can show you messages in the admin and, for example when an invoice is open, put the installation or one space into a restricted mode. Nobody can change these from inside the admin, administrators included; they come from the provider.

## Banners

Banners appear in a strip right below the top bar. Their colour says how urgent they are: information, a warning (yellow) or something urgent (red). The most urgent come first.

- A banner may end with **Learn more**, which opens the provider's page in a new tab.
- A banner with an **x** on the right can be closed. It stays closed for your account, on every browser and device you sign in on.
- Some banners are meant for administrators only, so colleagues may see fewer of them than you.

## The help menu

When the provider has set them, a **?** button in the top bar opens a menu with links to documentation, support, billing and upgrade pages. The billing link is shown to administrators only.

## Read-only

A read-only installation or space shows a yellow notice below the top bar: "This instance is read-only." or "This space is read-only.", usually followed by the reason.

| Still works | Does not work until it is lifted |
| --- | --- |
| Signing in and out | Saving, publishing and deleting content |
| Opening and reading every page | Uploading files |
| Exporting a space | Changing types, menus, designs, workflows, webhooks, members and settings |
| Your profile, password and notification settings | Workflows starting, and incoming webhooks |
| The website and the API | Forms on the designed site (they say "This form is unavailable right now") |

When you try to save, the admin says "This is read-only at the moment - changes are not saved." Nothing you already made is lost. When the provider lifts it, everything works again; reload the page if the notice is still showing.

A read-only space only affects that space: other spaces work normally.

## Suspended

A suspended installation shows a single page after you sign in, for everyone, administrators included. It says why, and shows the provider's help links.

While it is suspended, the website and the API are unavailable: visitors see a neutral "Temporarily unavailable" page. Nothing is deleted.

Contact your provider through the links on the page. Once they lift the suspension, click **Check again** (or just come back to the tab) and the admin opens as usual. **Sign out** signs you out.
